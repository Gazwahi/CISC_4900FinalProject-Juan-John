Funeral Planning Checklist

This checklist can help you better understand the elements of a funeral, guide your decisions, and help you feel more prepared.

  • Select a funeral service location: chapel, church, or gravesite
  • Decide on the time and date of service
  • Select a cemetery for burial
  • For those who are eligible, decide whether you want military honors
  • Determine the preparation of the deceased
  • Choose traditional clothing if needed
  • Select a casket
  • Prepare vital information such as date of birth and parents’ names
  • Have military discharge papers ready, if applicable
  • Determine how many death certificates you may need
  • Prepare information for death notices and obituary details
  • Decide how many limousines may be required
  • Decide whether to order personalized acknowledgement cards
  • Be prepared to sign a contract and authorizations
  • Be prepared to choose a payment method for services
